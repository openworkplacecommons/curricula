# Introduction to AI at Work

This is **illustrative material only**. Organizations should apply their own policies about permitted AI systems and confidential information.

## Learning goals

- Recognize that generated outputs can contain inaccuracies.
- Identify cases where company or personal information should not be shared with an AI service.

## Practice scenario

Before pasting an internal customer report into an AI assistant, check the organization's approved tool and information-handling policies.
